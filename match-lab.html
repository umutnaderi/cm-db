      Each probe resolves the outcome of an action you've already picked below — a cross that's already been delivered, a pass that's already arrived. It doesn't ask the engine to choose the action itself; that's what Free Play does.

      Whoever you assign to a role here is a forced participant in the probe, regardless of where you place them on the pitch or which team they're on — unlike Free Play, marker distance and team don't remove or add anyone.
