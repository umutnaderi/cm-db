Seed —

Match Setup

Choose the squads, format and start of play here, then press Apply Setup. Nothing on this panel touches the pitch until you do — it edits a draft, so an experiment that goes wrong leaves the current setup exactly as it was. Formation and instructions are in the main Tactics tab.

Squads

Format

Reduced-sided shapes are projected deterministically from the chosen formation — the same squad and formation always give the same XI. A format button never rolls a random side.

A neutral default shape, not a claim of historical authenticity — the catalogue carries no per-squad tactical metadata yet. Change it freely.

Select a player to display the role's likely operating area.

Select a marker to edit that position.

The choices follow this exact positional slot.

Goalkeepers use distribution and sweeping instructions. Shooting and tempo do not apply to them.

Player instructions inherit the team plan until explicitly overridden. Roles, duties and WIB/WOB positions guide the engine; live jobs, attributes and movement timing still decide what the player can execute.

              Each probe resolves the outcome of an action you've
              already picked below — a cross that's already been delivered, a
              pass that's already arrived. It doesn't ask the engine to choose
              the action itself; that's what Free Play does.
            
      
            
      
              Whoever you assign to a role here is a forced participant in
              the probe, regardless of where you place them on the pitch or
              which team they're on — unlike Free Play, marker distance and
              team don't remove or add anyone. Free Play's ball-ownership
              controls are hidden in this mode because they don't mean
              anything here: the ball is drawn at whichever placed player
              fills the scenario's own primary role below.
